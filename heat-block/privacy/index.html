Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

HEAT BLOCK is a free block-puzzle game published by HAT99 Studios. This policy explains how information may be processed when you play the game, use Apple Game Center, or choose to watch an optional rewarded advertisement.

Information processed by the game

HEAT BLOCK does not require you to create a HAT99 Studios account. Gameplay information, such as your current game state and scores, may be stored on your device to provide game functionality.

Optional advertising (Google AdMob)

HEAT BLOCK offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, other app interaction information, crash logs and performance data. This information may be used for advertising, measurement, analytics, security, fraud prevention, and service improvement, depending on the applicable settings and consent choices.

For details about Google's handling of data, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob iOS data disclosures.

Advertising consent and choices

Where required, HEAT BLOCK requests applicable advertising consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You may choose not to watch a rewarded ad. Where a privacy-options control is available within the game, you can use it to review your advertising choices. You can also manage applicable privacy and tracking permissions in your iPhone settings.

Apple Game Center

If you use Apple Game Center, Apple processes Game Center account and leaderboard information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. HEAT BLOCK uses Game Center to submit and display scores and leaderboards. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing your result

HEAT BLOCK may let you create and share a result image using the iOS share sheet. Sharing is initiated by you. Information you choose to send to another app or recipient is handled according to that destination's privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Game information stored locally may be removed by deleting the app, subject to any iOS backups. Game Center information is managed through Apple's services. Google and its partners control retention of data processed through their advertising services in accordance with their applicable policies. For questions about information processed directly by HAT99 Studios, contact us using the support page below.

Children

HEAT BLOCK is not specifically directed at children. Advertising and privacy choices may depend on region, age-related settings, and applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game or third-party services change. The latest version will be posted at this address with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit HEAT BLOCK Support.
