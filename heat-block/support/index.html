Frequently Asked Questions

Is HEAT BLOCK free?

Yes. HEAT BLOCK is free to play. Rewarded advertisements are optional.

How do I play?

Place blocks on the 9x9 board, clear complete rows and columns, and manage the heat to keep your game going.

How do the leaderboards work?

HEAT BLOCK uses Apple Game Center to display high scores and competitive leaderboards. Sign in to Game Center on your device to participate.

What is NO REVIVE WORLD RECORD?

It is a leaderboard for high scores achieved without using an ad-supported continue.

Are advertisements required?

No. Advertisements are optional and offer an additional chance to continue playing.
